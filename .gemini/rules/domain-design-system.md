# Project-Specific Design System: Apex Automotive Detailing & Ceramic Lab

## Visual Theme: Obsidian Carbon & Liquid Ceramic Gold
This project is an elite automotive detailing and PPF studio CRM. The UI must reflect the atmosphere of an exotic supercar detailing bay (Lamborghini, Porsche, Ferrari, BMW M Studio):

- **Surfaces:**
  - `carbon-950` (`#07080B`): Studio darkroom floor with subtle radial ambient glow.
  - `carbon-900` (`#0E1017`): High-gloss carbon fiber bay panels.
  - `carbon-850` (`#151822`): Elevated glass card surfaces.
  - `carbon-800` (`#1F2432`): Titanium hairline borders.
- **Primary Brand Accents:**
  - `ceramic-500` / `gold` (`#F59E0B`, `#FBBF24`): Liquid Ceramic Coating & Curing Heat Amber.
- **Secondary Accents:**
  - `hydro-500` (`#06B6D4`): Hydrophobic water-bead cyan for active bays & wash stages.
  - `racing-500` (`#F43F5E`): Brembo brake caliper red for urgent deliveries and alerts.
- **Typography & Details:**
  - High-precision badges with subtle glass borders.
  - Metallic gold gradients (`gold-gradient-text`) for headers and key totals.
